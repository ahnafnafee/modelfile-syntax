# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be respectful.
- Assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Disagreements are fine; ad hominem is not.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this expectation, contact the maintainer at the email on [ahnafnafee.dev](https://www.ahnafnafee.dev). Reports will be handled confidentially.
